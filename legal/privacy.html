Global Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026 • Last Updated: October 2026

🛡️ PRIVACY BY DESIGN: ZERO TELEMETRY STORAGE

Tesla Sound Simulator is engineered with a strict Local-First & Zero-Tracking Architecture. Your vehicle speeds, GPS coordinates, acceleration data, and audio preferences are calculated 100% locally in your device's browser memory. Driving data is never uploaded, stored, or sold to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

Because privacy is our core principle, we collect the bare minimum data required to deliver the service:

  • Local Audio & Interface Preferences: Saved strictly on your local device in localStorage (e.g., chosen car model, volume level, language setting, sound profile).
  • Sensor Data (GPS & Accelerometer): Processed in real time by your browser's Geolocation and DeviceMotion APIs solely to modulate audio engine RPM. This data never leaves your device.
  • Peer-to-Peer Telemetry (P2P): When linking your smartphone to your Tesla display, data travels directly between devices via end-to-end encrypted WebRTC DataChannels. The signaling server facilitates only the initial handshake and does not inspect or store telemetry payloads.
  • Subscription & Payment Processing: When purchasing a subscription ($5/mo or $50/yr), payment transactions are handled directly by PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateways (e.g., Stripe / Paddle). We never store or handle your credit card numbers.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use intrusive cross-site advertising cookies. We utilize browser localStorage to remember your selected language, audio presets, and active subscription token.

3. GDPR & CCPA Rights

Under GDPR (Articles 15-22) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to request access, rectification, or erasure of any personal data. Since we do not retain identifiable personal driving history, you can clear all local data at any time by clearing your browser cache and localStorage.

4. Children's Privacy

Our service is directed at licensed drivers and automotive enthusiasts. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

5. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries, data subject requests, or questions, please email our Data Protection Officer at: privacy@teslasound.app
