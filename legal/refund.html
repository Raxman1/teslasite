Subscription Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026 • Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview of Plans

Tesla Sound Simulator offers two straightforward subscription options:

  • Monthly Subscription ($5.00/month): Charged every month. Designed for flexible month-to-month access.
  • Annual Subscription ($50.00/year): Charged once annually, granting full access with 2 months free ($60 regular value for $50).

2. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee (Annual Plan)

If you purchase an Annual Subscription ($50/year) and are not completely satisfied with the audio fidelity, latency, or vehicle compatibility, you are entitled to a 100% full refund within 14 calendar days of the initial purchase date, no questions asked.

3. Monthly Subscription Policy

Due to the immediate digital delivery and consumption of high-fidelity procedural audio DSP algorithms, monthly subscriptions ($5/mo) are non-refundable once the billing month commences. However, you can cancel renewal at any second with 1 click.

4. Easy Cancellation at Any Time

You can cancel your subscription at any time without fees, penalties, or waiting periods. Upon cancellation:

  • You will continue to have full access to PRO features until the end of your current prepaid billing cycle.
  • Your card will not be charged again after the current billing cycle expires.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund under our 14-day guarantee, simply send an email to billing@teslasound.app with your order ID or email address associated with the purchase. Refunds are processed within 2–5 business days directly back to your original payment method.
